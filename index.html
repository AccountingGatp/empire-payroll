    How it works
    
    
      
      
    1. The Payentry PDF is read in this browser. Each employee's salary (SAL), regular (REG), employer tax, employee tax, deductions and net pay are extracted.
      2. 
      
    2. Each employee is matched to the Employee List to get the class. Unknown employees are booked provisionally by their Payentry department and flagged.
      3. 
      
    3. The rules in the SOP decide the account: officers 66010, city managers 66000.1 / 67610.1, Admin 66020 / 67610, everyone else 50005 Contract labor. Employer tax follows the pay. Where pay is both salary and regular, tax is split by share of gross.
      4. 
      
    4. Credits are net pay and employee plus employer taxes to 21040, with each employee's class. Employee deductions are a credit to 60100.
      5. 
      
    5. The checks compare the result with the report's own totals. If a red check fails, downloads stay off.
      6.
