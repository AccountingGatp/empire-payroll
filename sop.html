Empire US Payroll (Payentry) – SOP

Version of 23 Oct 2026. The app follows this document. Step numbers are the same as in the Word SOP.

Purpose: record each weekly US payroll for Empire Tours in QuickBooks Online (QBO). Follow the steps in order. If anything is not covered, stop and ask Jonathan Yaffe (jyaffe@empire-tp.com) rather than guessing.

You need before you start: access to VM1 and Splashtop (GATP-Win11), the Credentials_Empire Google Sheet for the Payentry login, the latest Employee List, and access to QBO.

What the app does for you: Part B (check names), Part C (amounts), the lines of Part D and the checks of Part F. You still do by hand: Part A (get the report, the verification code comes from Palak’s phone), entering or importing the journal in QBO, and the emails.

Part A – Get the payroll report

  1. Log in to the remote computer (Splashtop). Payentry is used from GATP-Win11, not from your own PC.
    1. Log in to VM1. On the desktop, double-click Splashtop Business (if already open, skip ahead).
    2. Under Default Group, double-click GATP-Win11.
    3. Wait on “Connecting…” until the Windows sign-in screen appears.
    4. On the sign-in screen (user GATP), click Sign in. No password is needed.
    5. Open Google Chrome on GATP-Win11.
  2. Log in to Payentry.
    1. Open the Google Sheet Credentials_Empire and find the row PayEntry (payroll login): username, password, Company ID and the note about the authentication app. Do not copy these into the SOP, the app or emails.
    2. In a new Chrome tab on GATP-Win11, go to new.payentry.com/107/Account/Login.
    3. Enter the username, password and Company ID and click LOGIN.
    4. On Enter Verification Code, ask Palak Soni Ma’am for the current code (it comes from the authentication app on her phone). Ask only when you are ready to type it, then click Verify Code.
  3. Go to Reports, then Company Reports.
  4. Select Labor, click Search, then Run.
  5. Choose the date range (the pay date you are recording) and run the report.
  6. Go to My Reports and open Labor Distribution Report – PDF. Save it to the client folder, named by pay date.

The Word SOP has no step 7; numbering continues at 8.

Part B – Check every employee against the Employee List

Do this before booking anything. The app does it automatically when you drop the report and the Employee List.

  1. Write down every employee name on the Labor Distribution Report.
  2. Look each name up in the Employee List. It shows the employee’s class (Chicago, Admin, See it All, NYC, Washington D.C.).
  3. If every name is on the list, go to Part C.
  4. If any employee is NOT on the list:
    1. Highlight the name and list all missing employees (name, pay type on the report, weekly gross, and what city or role you know).
    2. Do not guess a class.
    3. Email Jonathan (copy empire@gatpsolutions.com) with the list and ask for (a) the class and (b) whether any pay is city-manager salary and from what date. The app writes this email for you.
    4. When Jonathan replies, add the employee to the Employee List with the confirmed class, then continue. If you must close the books before he replies, book the rest of the payroll and leave the missing employee’s amounts in a clearly labelled hold line, so nothing is posted to a wrong class.
  5. Also check the special cases (Part E) for each name. Some employees are always coded a certain way regardless of the Employee List.

In the app: employees not on the list are included in the journal under a temporary class (from their Payentry department), marked [class unconfirmed], and the app drafts the email to Jonathan.

  3. Employer tax follows the pay (Jonathan, 15 Oct). Pay to Contract labor: its employer tax goes to Contract labor in the same class. City-manager salary: employer tax to 67610.1. Only Admin pay keeps its tax in 67610.
  4. Splitting tax between salary and hourly: if Payentry shows them on separate pages, use each page’s actual tax total. If it combines them on one page, split the combined employer tax in proportion to each pay type’s share of gross pay for that week (agreed with Jonathan, 28 Sep).
  5. Employee withholdings are never an expense. Only the employer tax is expensed. Employee taxes go to the liability line with the net pay.
  6. Total by class. Add up each line by class (do not post employee by employee). Keep a per-person working for the city-manager amounts so they tie back to the report by name. The app’s Excel file has a “By class” sheet and a “City manager” sheet for this.

Part D – Record the journal entry in QBO

  1. Create one journal entry per pay date, dated the pay date.
  2. Enter the debit lines from the table in step 14, one line per account per class.
  3. Enter the credit lines: 21040 Accrued Payroll Liabilities for the total employer plus employee taxes, and 21040 again for the total net pay.
  4. Set the class on every line. Class names must match QBO exactly (for example Empire Tours:Chicago; NYC is a standalone class).
  5. Check that total debits equal total credits. Save the entry.
  6. Handle the people paid outside Payentry (Jared Wiseman direct payments; Deel payroll for Yassine, Jennifer and Louise) as described in Part E. In the app: the optional box “Add payments made outside Payentry”.

Part F – Final checks

  1. Total salary plus regular pay by class equals the report’s gross pay for the pay date.
  2. Debits equal credits, and every class exists in QBO.
  3. Every employee on the report is on the Employee List, or has been sent to Jonathan for confirmation.
  4. The monthly City Manager List shared with Jonathan is updated and ties to QBO (66000.1 and 67610.1). The app does not build this list yet; use its “City manager” sheet for the weekly numbers.
  5. Reply to Louise (copy Jonathan) to confirm the payroll is recorded. Raise open questions on the weekly call.

Open items to confirm with Jonathan

  • Charleston and Washington D.C. classes exist in QBO with the exact names.
  • Adam Sherman’s start date (14 Aug or 1 Aug).
  • Dates for Griffin Conaty’s move to Charleston and then another city.
  • That the Deel payroll summaries are booked at gross down to net.
